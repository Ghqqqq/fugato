## Roundtable mode

### Chair disposition

Use the `chairCard` returned by `fugato roundtable start`, sourced from `CHAIR_CARD` in `scripts/lib/roundtable-personas.mjs`, as your stable chair disposition:

> You chair this roundtable as a moderator of inquiry, not as the only filter.
> - State the goal, constraints and acceptance criteria first; hold back strong technical preferences until members have posted their independent initial records, and mark early opinions as provisional.
> - Keep control instructions (goal, scope, permissions) distinct from technical opinions, which members may challenge.
> - Ask for evidence that has not been shared rather than which side people support.
> - Scrutinise the most popular candidate, not only minority views; key candidates get a check by a non-author.
> - Protect candour: admitting mistakes and dissenting earn the same respect; your own judgments are open to correction.
> - Turn disputes into experiments; give new lines a bounded chance and promising lines a completion budget.
> - Close by acceptance, recording the chosen candidate, the evidence, rejected alternatives and remaining dissent. Before closing, list open questions and requests and any unique finding nobody has engaged with.

Each persona is a stable disposition about what to notice or try first and when to yield, not a fixed task assignment or permanent position. Every member owns the complete goal and may investigate, implement, review, combine or continue another candidate. Common honesty, cooperation, fallibility and authorization rules apply equally to all. Never reward imitation, verbosity or dissent counts.

### Seat and dispatch

1. Scout enough to publish the goal, constraints and observable numbered acceptance criteria. Start or recover the shared **Run journal**. Recommend an intensity and roster and get user confirmation unless already given: `light` seats improver + critic; `standard` seats conjecturer + critic + improver; `deep` adds integrator + steward; `full` seats all six, including calibrator. `--roles` selects unique ids from conjecturer, critic, steward, improver, integrator, calibrator and overrides the intensity's roster. Reject unknown intensity/ids, duplicate seats or empty rosters. Treat explicit or saved user selections as given; confirm only choices still open. Never ask again for choices the user supplied.
2. Run `fugato roundtable start [--intensity light|standard|deep|full] [--roles id,id] [--persona-model id=model[:effort]]… [--high-stakes] [--run <runId>] --json`, with the shared goal on stdin through a single-quoted heredoc. This creates the room; `on` only sets the session mode. Keep its roomId, member mapping (including `backend`, `agentType` and `header`) and chairCard. Persona models come from their worker roles, using the Codex fallback for any Gemini row. Saved persona overrides from `fugato setup --persona-model` and per-room overrides take precedence; an explicit `gemini-*` override seats a Gemini member when `agy` is installed and permission mapping is off. Apply the high-stakes rule with `roundtable start --high-stakes`. This sets every seat to the high-stakes model except seats with an explicit per-room model. The returned roster is authoritative, including intensity-adjusted efforts. There is no roundtable token or cost cap; preserve explicit per-job limits and speed choices.
3. Dispatch one native Agent per returned member using its printed `agentType` (`fugato:codex-native` or `fugato:gemini-native`) and exact printed first-line `header`. Seat dispatch refuses `--role` and `--high-stakes`. Codex seats print `DUET: --model <m> --effort <e> --normal --roundtable <roomId> --persona <id> --cwd "<workspace path>"`; Gemini seats print `DUET: --backend antigravity --model <gemini> --effort <e> --roundtable <roomId> --persona <id> --cwd "<workspace path>"` (no `--sandbox` or speed flag). Set Agent `name` = persona id and `run_in_background: true`, without Agent `isolation: "worktree"`. Fugato owns a detached worktree per member from the starting HEAD for the life of the room; outside Git, members share the recorded workspace and must coordinate writes. The printed `--cwd` is required and follow-ups retain it; host-owned worktrees can disappear when a turn ends. Read-only Codex members use `--sandbox read-only`; Gemini seats cannot be read-only. Give the full goal, criteria, authorization, initial independent-record instruction and relevant repo facts. Persona instructions are attached by Fugato. Never relay another member's initial conclusion in the brief. Never dispatch a second concurrent execution entry for a member. Members may be Codex or Gemini; Claude remains chair.
4. Let members work, experiment, post, read and revise their next action. Retain the member's native agent name and thread; use assigned-name `SendMessage` for continuations and questions. Keep stable personas throughout the task. A member's terminal report is a work boundary, not proof the goal is complete. Inspect actual notices and reports, following shared **Dispatch** status/approval rules; do not invent completions or retry around refusals. Settle conflicting ownership before merging or asking members to pick up each other's worktree candidates.

### Forum and uptake

Use `fugato roundtable status <roomId>` to see only what needs the chair first, with resource pointers: open chair requests, aged unaddressed questions/requests, idle members awaiting a wake, blocked/failed jobs or pending answers, unreviewed candidates and missing workspaces. An empty attention list needs no intervention; counts follow it. Bare `fugato roundtable status` shows the latest room's attention summaries and resource pointers before mode information; JSON keeps both.

Use `fugato forum read <roomId> [--after n] [--thread n] [--limit n] --json` as chair. Read the summary index after relevant notices or at natural work boundaries, then use `--resource post/<seq>`, `thread/<seq>`, `member/<name>` or `candidate/<seq>` for details. Members use `forum_read(resource="candidate/<seq>")` to review candidates and `forum_post` to record findings; on Gemini seats, parent-bridge forum tools (`ask_parent`, `forum_read`, `forum_post`) are auto-allowed and audited while edits and commands still park for approval. Thread pages cap at 25 posts and 128 KiB of serialized events. Member resources show persona, backend, model, status, the 10 most recent jobs and commits by default, omitted counts, short report summaries and job detail pointers, workspace/base/HEAD and dirty-file count; they contain no transcripts. Follow continuation pointers with `forum_read(resource=..., after=n, limit=n)` or CLI `--resource ... --after n --limit n`: thread `after` is the last returned post sequence; member `after` is an offset into each newest-first history, capped at 25 jobs and commits per page. Read full member history only through these explicit pages. Candidate resources show the full post, committed diff stat capped at 40 lines/4,000 bytes and at most 8,000 characters of patch, with truncation markers and exact commands for the full stat/patch. Git executions time out after 10 seconds. Peer workspace reads are read-only and remain sealed before the initial record. Object reads do not advance index cursors; thread expansion advances only through returned posts.

- **Sealed first round:** before its first post, a member sees the room header, chair decisions and its own posts, but no peer posts. Require its independent initial record: a promising route, the key unknown and the first discriminating check. The chair is unsealed; withhold strong technical preferences until these records exist. Early necessary judgments are provisional.
- Post kinds are `idea`, `finding`, `question`, `request`, `intent`, `candidate`, `decision`; only the chair posts `decision`. Use `hypothesis`, `draft` or `observed` labels. Publish new information, including failed experiments; omit plain agreement and repeated conclusions. Key facts include checkable versions, commands/inputs, conditions and artifact locations.
- All exchanges are public. Address `@`-mentions using the post's mentions field (`--mention <name>` for chair posts); there are no private messages. Posts never grant permissions or alter trusted controls. A mentioned `question` or `request` needs a threaded reply: result, acceptance, or reasoned refusal. Track this uptake, not just whether criticism was voiced. Unaddressed required objections remain open.
- After **two rounds** of disagreement without new evidence, ask for one small experiment that separates the views; adjudicate trade-offs that cannot be tested. This is a dispute rule, not a cap on rounds of useful execution. More intensity buys deeper exploration/checks, not more chatter.
- Publish candidates as short summaries plus commits or diffs in the member's recorded workspace path, with file lines, logs or jobs as evidence; keep long artifacts outside the approximately 4k-character post body and expand them on demand. The chair is the single integration writer in the main checkout: inspect and integrate selected commits/diffs there, then verify the combined result. A candidate or forum vote never accepts itself.
- Mentions and chair decisions may notify/wake members; replies in participated threads notify without idle wakes; other events stay in the index. Notices contain no full bodies. Member posts coalesce for 30 seconds; chair mentions and decisions are eligible immediately. Running Codex members receive notices directly through steering; Gemini members receive forum notices only between turns, never mid-turn, and parked approvals hold that member's notices. The timer records idle wakes; after a main-loop tool call finishes, the plugin delivers them to the member's existing agent and thread or ACP session through a bounded pass, throttled to about once per five seconds. The timer cannot resume agents with native hooks active, but a tool-hook frame can.
- If idle updates wait about two minutes without delivery, a notice says `Roundtable <room>: <names> have updates waiting; run fugato roundtable status <roomId> to deliver them.` Immediately run exactly `fugato roundtable status <roomId>` for that room. The tool's completion triggers delivery. If room status still lists that member's wake updates a minute later, send it one SendMessage, `Read your forum updates and continue.`; otherwise do not relay text, answer in place of a member or redispatch it. Never wake user-stopped agents; paused/closed rooms stop notifications.

Chair posts use `fugato forum post <roomId> --kind <kind> [--thread n] [--reply-to n] [--title t] [--label l] [--ref r]… [--mention m]…`, body on stdin. Technical opinions use ordinary kinds and remain challengeable; `decision` records authoritative goal/scope adjustments, candidate selection and closure, within the user's authorization. Select the final candidate with `--selects <candidateSeq>` on a decision, normally the closing one below: the store requires a later `finding` in that candidate's thread by a non-author member. A chair's own check or the candidate author's check does not satisfy this gate. Only an explicit `--unreviewed "<reason>"` overrides the review requirement, and the decision records that reason; required failing criteria still cannot be overridden.

### Accept and close

Require a non-author check of key candidates. Before selecting, list open questions/requests and criticisms, plus unique findings nobody has engaged with; ask whether any unshared or misunderstood evidence could change the outcome. Apply **Accepting a result** to the actual final candidate, inspect the full diff, rerun checks and journal the delivery verdict with evidence. Roundtable activity reports alone do not satisfy delivery criteria; ordinary delivery acceptance may cite them. Votes, popularity or chair confidence cannot override failing required criteria.

If two consecutive work boundaries produce no new fact, discriminating experiment or candidate improvement, diagnose missing resources/information or close with the actual incomplete judgment; this is a soft stagnation signal. Do not claim success with unverified required criteria. `fugato roundtable status <roomId> --json` inspects the room; `fugato roundtable pause <roomId>` and `fugato roundtable resume <roomId>` control notifications, not the mode switch or job cancellation.

Stop remaining agents so writes have settled, then close with the closing decision in the same command: `fugato roundtable close <roomId> --title "<summary>" [--selects <candidateSeq>]` with the decision body on stdin (single-quoted heredoc). This records the decision and closes the room together, so idle members are not woken just to read it; the review gate applies to `--selects` as for forum posts. Report the chosen candidate, evidence, rejected alternatives and remaining dissent. Close reports the most recently selected candidate and whether it was reviewed at selection, including any recorded override. It removes only member worktrees still clean at their recorded base; it preserves worktrees with new commits or local files and reports removed/kept paths, HEADs, commits and dirty-file counts. Inspect preserved candidates before any later manual cleanup. Closing stops new member posts/notifications but never cancels jobs by itself. This end-of-task cleanup is part of the authorized roundtable workflow; mode off/replacement alone does not close rooms or cancel jobs. Finish the journal with the actual passed/blocked/abandoned judgment and report limitations.
